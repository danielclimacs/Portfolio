What To Expect...

Looped is the future of all college applications, designed to simplify and enhance the everyday university experience for students and staff.
Built in response to the failings and frustrations of current college-based applications, Looped reinvents the digital space of universities with a focus on efficiency, integration, and user-friendliness.
With this, come a lot of expectations we hope to deliver on, such as what you will see below.

Expected Features:

  • An all-inclusive calendar: Course schedules, assignment due dates, and event reminders.
  • An in-app E-Wallet: Pay for tuition, clubs & societies, gym, campus merchandise, and manage student IDs.
  • AI-powered personal assistant: Academic reminders, suggestions, and multi-language support for international students.
  • A hub for services: Accommodation, Parking, Clubs & Societies, and The Library.
  • A modern interface: Minimalistic, user-friendly design for both Android and iOS devices.

Revenue and Scaling:

Revenue Model

  • Annual Licensing: Universities pay a fee based on student population (approx. €3 per student).
  • Transaction Fees: 1.5% commission on in-app payments for events, campus merchandise, and services.
  • Data Insights: Optional analytics reporting for institutions to enhance student engagement.
