Who Is The App For?

Looped is a college app specially designed to streamline and enhance the daily experiences of both students and lecturers.

What is in it For The Students?

For students, the app simplifies everyday challenges like keeping track of assignment deadlines and schedules, while improving the way that students can communicate with their lecturers.

How Can Looped Help Lecturers?

The app provides a more efficient way for lecturers and students to communicate with one another via our messaging system. This can help lecturers streamline their teaching responsibilities and improve student engagement.
