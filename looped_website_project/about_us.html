About Us

We are a team of passionate individuals working to revolutionize the university experience for students with our innovative app, Looped. Our mission is to create a more efficient, user-friendly, and integrated platform that addresses the frustrations of current college-based applications.

Our Idea

College students rely heavily on digital tools, and while many university apps exist, they often fall short of expectations.
Looped is designed to improve upon current offerings by focusing on usability, integration, and efficiency. We aim to enhance the college experience by bringing everything students need into one intuitive platform.
