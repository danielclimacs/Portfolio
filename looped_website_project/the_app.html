The App

Looped - Student Life, Made Simple
Say goodbye to the chaos of multiple university applications, websites and services.
Looped allows staff and students access to their college lives within a single, secure interface.
Everything you need, in just one place.

The Design:

Inspired by simple, clean and minimalistic yet modern designs, Looped features a intuitive and friendly UI.
Users are given the opportunity to customize their dashboard, toggle between light and dark mode,
access key tools necessary to optimize productivity and also edit widgets and tabs in favour of preference or importance.

  • Navigation that prioritizes ease-of-use and mirrors familiar navigational patterns seen on other mobile applications.
  • University branded themes & colour palettes.
  • Accessibility and inclusivity driven designs.

Technology Behind The App:

To allow Looped to reach its full potential we had to put lots of thought into the technology that would maintain our application.
To which these we found the most relevant:

  • AWS Cloud Hosting, a secure and scalable cloud infrastructure.
  • Single Sign-On Authentication (SSO), for fast and easy access.
  • Progressive Web App (PWA) support for offline mode, to access key tools and features in times of need.
  • Usage analytics, which would available for the administration universities if sought after.
